# End-to-End User Workflows

### Workflow 1: Resume Upload & ATS Audit
1. Navigate to **Resume Center** (`/resumes`).
2. Upload a PDF/DOCX resume file.
3. Review the 6-dimension scoring breakdown at `/resumes/analyze`.

### Workflow 2: Opportunity Matching & CRM Tracking
1. Open **Job Matcher** (`/jobs`).
2. Filter by role or location.
3. Click **Save to CRM** to track in the Kanban board (`/applications`).

### Workflow 3: Interview Preparation & Practice
1. Open **Mock Interview** (`/interviews`).
2. Generate role-specific questions.
3. Submit a practice answer and receive instant rubric grading.
