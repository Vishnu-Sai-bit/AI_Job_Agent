# AI Job Copilot & Career Intelligence Platform - Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
The AI Job Copilot & Career Intelligence Platform is a full-stack career acceleration ecosystem built in Python and Flask. It consolidates job discovery, 6-dimension ATS resume audits, tailored application CRM tracking, mock interview simulation, and market skill deficit analytics into a unified environment.

## 2. Core Capabilities
1. **Dynamic Opportunity Discovery**: Ingests, normalizes, deduplicates, and ranks listings from verified job boards.
2. **6-Dimension ATS Audit**: Scores Contact, Skills, Experience, Education, Projects, and Certifications.
3. **Application CRM Pipeline**: Tracks applications across Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, and Rejected stages with automated recruiter follow-ups.
4. **Mock Interview Room**: Generates technical and behavioral questions with instant AI rubric evaluation.
5. **Career Intelligence & Learning Roadmaps**: Identifies market skill gaps and produces weekly milestone pathways.
